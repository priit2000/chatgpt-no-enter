Name:
Disable Enter in ChatGPT

Summary (132 chars max):
Prevents accidental sends in ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, and Grok. Enter = newline, Ctrl+Enter = send.

Category:
Productivity

Language:
English

Description:
Tired of accidentally sending half-finished prompts?

This extension swaps the Enter key behavior in AI chat apps so you can compose multi-line messages without fear of premature sends.

How it works:
- Enter inserts a new line (no more accidental sends)
- Ctrl+Enter (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Enter (macOS) sends your message
- Shift+Enter still works as a newline too

Supported platforms:
- ChatGPT (chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com)
- Google Gemini (gemini.google.com)
- Anthropic Claude (claude.ai)
- Microsoft Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com)
- xAI Grok (grok.com)

Lightweight, no permissions beyond the supported sites, no data collection. Just a simple quality-of-life fix for anyone who writes long prompts.

Works on Chrome, Brave, Edge, and other Chromium-based browsers.

Privacy:
This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. It only runs on the listed AI chat sites and modifies keyboard behavior locally in the browser.

Single purpose:
Changes Enter key from "send message" to "new line" in AI chat applications, requiring Ctrl+Enter or Cmd+Enter to send instead.
